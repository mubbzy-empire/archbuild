# ArchVision Professional — Phase 12

## Construction-grade geometry & coordination

Phase 12 advances the canonical Building IR into a construction-authoring layer.

### Added
- Layered wall assembly metadata and derived construction layers.
- Construction-aware opening families with host wall relationships, frame/reveal/glazing metadata and leaf counts.
- Calculated stair construction records with risers, treads, run, headroom and guard/handrail intent.
- Roof framing records for rafters/ridge members derived from the top footprint.
- MEP route fitting/connection records and deterministic proximity coordination checks.
- Construction geometry group for wall layers, roof framing, stair handrail/guard intent and MEP fitting markers.
- Associative regeneration after model edits so hosted openings, construction data and coordination data are refreshed from the canonical model.
- Phase 12 QA and construction manifest export.
- Professional Modeler CONSTRUCTION inspector panel.

### Design boundary
Phase 12 geometry and coordination are authoring/coordination aids, not structural engineering calculations, code certification or permit approval.
